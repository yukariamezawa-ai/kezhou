Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download AI videos and camera movement prompts on Android?

Simply open Lumen, browse to your favorite platform (Sora, Kling, Runway, Twitter), and tap the floating Inspo Capsule button. The video, cover thumbnail, and camera prompt tags are automatically captured to your local Inspo Board without subscription traps.

Why don't downloaded videos appear in my main phone gallery?

This is Lumen's core privacy feature. Research clips and sensitive reference videos are stored in an isolated, encrypted sandbox. If you wish to make a file public, use the 'Export to System Gallery' option in the shot details menu.

Is Lumen 100% free?

Yes! Daily 1080P/720P high-speed video saving and local Inspo Board management are completely free forever. Optional Pro features can be unlocked for 24 hours simply by watching a sponsor clip, or with a lifetime pass.
